Web application · 2026

Ilia Plan: bilingual company website with a project-controls dashboard

A corporate website for a project management and project controls consultancy, in Persian (right-to-left) and English. The team edits services, articles, portfolio items and site settings from an admin panel, and visitors reach the company through a contact form with spam protection.

Its main feature is an interactive sample report: S-curve, manpower, material consumption, financial progress, a Gantt chart and a delay register. All figures come from the database, and indicators such as SPI and CPI are calculated from the weekly data.

I defined the requirements and design direction and built it with AI assistance (Claude). It ships with Docker, MariaDB and 22 automated tests.
